# Code scope and provenance

## Direct manuscript pipeline

- `common.py`, `config.yaml`: portable paths, reproducibility helpers, and shared configuration.
- `features/`, `neural/`: source-to-cache preprocessing and related transfer initialization.
- `phase2/engine.py`, `phase2/models.py`: datasets, grouped fitting, metrics, architectures, views, losses, augmentation, and PaPaGei integration.
- `phase2/registry.py`, `phase2/innovation200_registry.py`, `phase2/icbme_clinical_registry.py`: deterministic experiment definitions. Their committed JSON locks under `phase2/protocol/` are authoritative and are not regenerated by `run_all.py`.
- `phase2/run_search.py`, `run_foundation_adaptation.py`, `run_confirmation_cv.py`: broad pilot evidence.
- `phase2/run_icbme_*`, `analyze_icbme_*`: clinical, uncertainty, conformal, and dedicated event screens.
- `phase2/run_innovation200_*`, `analyze_innovation200_*`, `select_innovation200_finalists.py`: locked 200-method search and 75-fit confirmation.
- `phase2/prepare_external.py`, `run_external_validation.py`, `analyze_external_calibration.py`: cuff-labelled transfer studies.
- `phase2/prepare_vitaldb_synchronized_external.py`, `evaluate_innovation200_vitaldb.py`: synchronized invasive external stress test.
- `phase2/finalize_innovation200.py`: final baseline, event-safety, decision, and audit artifacts.

## Preserved exploratory provenance

The `phase2` directory also retains MIMIC-III auxiliary and UQVS scripts used while testing alternative directions. They are preserved for provenance, but they do not support the final manuscript's primary tables and are not part of `run_all.py`. Their source datasets are therefore not represented as final-paper data dependencies.

## Claim enforcement

The final paper's reported result values are registered under `claims/`; CI and local audit execute `scripts/verify_claims.py`. Changing a reported result without changing its source artifact and claim registry causes a failure.
